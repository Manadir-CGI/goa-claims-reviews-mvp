Design rationale

Advance Intelligence — a predictive view, not another queue

  • Why it exists: the QA queue answers “which claim do I review next?”. This answers a portfolio question the reviewers can’t see today — where is the advance out-running the actual claim, and what will next period need? Ease-of-review lever #1 (data-viz & insights) and #7 (Pattern Analyzer integration).
  • Advance-vs-actual reconciliation (12-month lines + recovery-gap band) and a recovery-rate bar chart against a 98% target — both interactive (hover readout + series toggle), per the chart-fidelity rule. Grounds the split-payment / advance-recovery vision (discovery §6).
  • Provider exposure table ranks by projected balance, worst-first, and expands to the recovery math + the model’s top driver + a right-sizing recommendation — surfacing the “Negative balance” risk before payment (guardrail: surface data-integrity / advance issues early). Modular per vehicle (badge + routing from a map).
  • Next-period forecast callout quantifies the projected shortfall and how much right-sizing the flagged advances recovers — decision support, never an auto-reject.
  • Numbers illustrative (G5). Real GoA components + semantic tokens throughout; the $1.4M exposure echoes the board’s FDH-scale figures as rationale only.
