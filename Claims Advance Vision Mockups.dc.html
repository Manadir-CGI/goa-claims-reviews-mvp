1AOperator DashboardKPI-led
Design rationale

Operator front door — KPI-led

  • Leads with status KPIs (§2.3 insights) so the operator instantly reads portfolio health.
Strengths
  • Whole-portfolio health at a glance — strong for operators with many claims / funding types.
  • KPI pattern is scannable and reusable across roles.
Trade-offs
  • The single advance can get lost among the KPIs.
  • More to parse for a small, single-stream operator.

Design rationale

Operator front door — single-focus advance

  • Foregrounds one advance card with the Paid → Recovered → Net mechanic (§2.1 transparency).
Strengths
  • Advance + recovery is unmissable → directly serves the transparency need.
  • Calmer, lower cognitive load (progressive disclosure, §2.5).
Trade-offs

  • Less at-a-glance portfolio context than 1A.

13Advance IntelligenceVision · predictive · exception-only
Design rationale

The future-state overlay on phases 1 & 4

  • Not a live step — it's the discovery's test of the resilience / foundational-model principle (§6), applied to the advance.
  • Evolves 10: since advance = avg(last 3), it's a lagging forecast — predictive drift recalibrates it before the mismatch surfaces in 11.
  • Evolves 11: trust-based auto-clear so only exceptions reach a human — actionable-flags + progressive-disclosure (§2.3, §2.5) taken to their conclusion.
  • Generalises the spine: the advance becomes a reusable recovery module in the FinOps factory, with payee as a variable (operator → family / educator).
  • Discovery role: a north-star probe to check the near-term FDH slice is building the right foundation — a talking artifact, not a build item.
